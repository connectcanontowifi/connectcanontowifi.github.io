Why Is My Canon Printer Not Connecting to WiFi?

Several things can prevent a Canon printer from connecting to WiFi. Common causes include:

  • Incorrect WiFi password
  • Weak wireless signal
  • Printer connected to a different network
  • Router problems
  • Unsupported WiFi frequency
  • Incorrect printer network settings
  • Outdated or missing Canon drivers
  • Printer firmware or software problems
  • Guest WiFi network isolation
  • VPN or firewall interference
  • Recently changed router or WiFi password
  • Temporary communication errors

Before making complicated changes, start with the basic connection checks.

Step 1: Turn On Your Canon Printer

Connect the printer to a power outlet and press the Power button.

Wait until the printer finishes starting up.

Make sure there are no error messages displayed on the printer.

If the printer shows an ink, paper, cartridge, or hardware error, resolve that issue before attempting wireless setup.

Step 2: Check Your WiFi Router

Before troubleshooting the printer, verify that your WiFi network is working.

Connect your phone or computer to the same WiFi network and open a website.

If other devices cannot access the internet, restart your router before continuing.

Also make sure the router is within reasonable range of the printer.

Step 3: Check Your WiFi Name and Password

Make sure you know the correct:

  • WiFi network name (SSID)
  • WiFi password

When entering the password into the printer, carefully check uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

Important: A single incorrect character can prevent the Canon printer from connecting.

Step 4: Open Wireless Setup on the Canon Printer

On compatible Canon printers, open the network or wireless settings.

Depending on your model, you may see options such as:

WiFi Setup

Wireless LAN Setup

Wireless Setup

Network Settings

Select the option that starts wireless configuration.

The exact menu names and button combinations vary by Canon printer model.

Step 5: Select Your WiFi Network

After starting wireless setup, the printer may search for nearby wireless networks.

Find your home or office WiFi network in the list.

Select the correct network.

If you don't see your network, move the printer closer to the router and search again.

Step 6: Enter the WiFi Password

Enter your wireless password when prompted.

Check the password carefully before confirming.

After submitting the password, wait for the printer to establish a connection.

A successful connection may be indicated by a WiFi icon, wireless status message, or network confirmation.

Step 7: Connect Canon Printer Using WPS

Some Canon printers support WPS, which can simplify wireless setup.

If both your router and printer support WPS:

  1. Turn on the Canon printer.
  2. Open the printer's wireless setup menu.
  3. Select WPS.
  4. Press the WPS button on your router when instructed.
  5. Wait for the printer to connect.
  6. Confirm the wireless connection.

If WPS doesn't work, use the printer's normal wireless setup method instead.

How to Connect a Canon Printer to WiFi Using a Computer

How to Connect Canon Printer to WiFi on Windows 11

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select Bluetooth & devices.
  3. Select Printers & scanners.
  4. Click Add device.
  5. Wait for Windows to search for available printers.
  6. Select your Canon printer.
  7. Follow the instructions to finish adding it.

If Windows cannot find the printer, check that your computer and printer are connected to the same WiFi network.

How to Connect Canon Printer to WiFi on Windows 10

For Windows 10:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select Devices.
  3. Click Printers & scanners.
  4. Select Add a printer or scanner.
  5. Wait for Windows to search.
  6. Select your Canon printer.
  7. Complete the installation.

If the printer isn't detected, install the appropriate Canon driver and try again.

How to Connect Canon Printer to WiFi on Mac

To add a compatible Canon wireless printer to a Mac:

  1. Connect the Mac to your WiFi network.
  2. Connect the Canon printer to the same network.
  3. Open System Settings.
  4. Select Printers & Scanners.
  5. Click Add Printer, Scanner, or Fax.
  6. Select your Canon printer.
  7. Click Add.
  8. Print a test page.

Canon Printer Not Connecting to WiFi: Top Fixes

1. Restart the Canon Printer

Turn off the printer.

Wait approximately 30 seconds.

Turn it back on and try Connecting to WiFi again.

A simple restart can clear temporary network communication problems.

2. Restart Your WiFi Router

Turn off your router.

Wait for about 30 seconds.

Turn it back on and allow it to reconnect to the internet.

Then try connecting your Canon printer again.

3. Verify the WiFi Password

Incorrect passwords are one of the simplest reasons a printer won't connect.

Check your password carefully and make sure you aren't accidentally entering the password for another network.

4. Move the Printer Closer to the Router

A weak WiFi signal can cause connection failures.

For initial setup, place the Canon printer closer to the router.

After the connection works, you can move the printer to its normal location.

5. Check 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Compatibility

Some Canon printer models support 2.4 GHz wireless networks but may not support 5 GHz networks.

If your router provides separate networks, try connecting the printer to the supported 2.4 GHz network.

If your router combines 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz under one network name, check the router and printer documentation for compatibility.

6. Don't Use a Guest Network

Guest networks can isolate connected devices.

Computer: HomeWiFi

Printer: HomeWiFi-Guest

The printer may have internet access but still be inaccessible from the computer.

Connect the printer and computer to the same primary network whenever possible.

7. Check the Printer's WiFi Indicator

Look at the printer's WiFi icon or network status.

If the wireless indicator is off, the printer may not be connected.

If it repeatedly flashes or shows an error, open the network settings and perform wireless setup again.

8. Reset the Canon Printer's Network Settings

If the printer previously connected to another router or WiFi network, old network information may interfere with setup.

Reset the printer's network settings according to your model's instructions.

  1. Start wireless setup again.
  2. Select your current WiFi network.
  3. Enter the current password.
  4. Confirm the connection.
  5. Add the printer to your computer again.

The exact network-reset procedure varies by Canon model.

Canon Printer Connected to WiFi but Computer Can't Find It

Sometimes the printer successfully connects to WiFi but doesn't appear on the computer.

Check the Networks

Make sure the printer and computer are connected to the same local network.

Restart Both Devices

Restart the printer and computer.

Check the Firewall

Windows Firewall or third-party security software can sometimes interfere with local printer discovery.

Disconnect VPN

A VPN can interfere with communication between your computer and devices on your local network.

Temporarily disconnect the VPN and check whether the printer appears.

Reinstall the Driver

Download and install the appropriate Canon printer driver for your exact model.

Canon Printer Connected to WiFi but Not Printing

A WiFi connection doesn't guarantee that printing will work.

  • Printer is powered on
  • Paper is loaded
  • Ink is available
  • Printer isn't displaying an error
  • Print queue isn't stuck
  • Correct printer is selected
  • Printer isn't offline
  • Correct driver is installed
  • Computer and printer are on the same network

Try printing a simple document after checking these items.

Canon Printer Keeps Disconnecting From WiFi

If the printer connects but repeatedly loses the wireless connection, try these steps:

  1. Move the printer closer to the router.
  2. Restart the router.
  3. Restart the printer.
  4. Check WiFi signal strength.
  5. Check whether the printer supports your wireless frequency.
  6. Avoid guest WiFi.
  7. Check router device-isolation settings.
  8. Update compatible Canon software or firmware.
  9. Reconfigure the printer's wireless connection.

If other devices also lose their WiFi connection, the problem may be with the router rather than the printer.

Canon Printer Won't Connect After Changing WiFi Password

If you recently changed your WiFi password, your Canon printer may still have the old password stored.

You'll need to reconnect it.

  1. Open the printer's wireless settings.
  2. Start wireless setup.
  3. Select the current WiFi network.
  4. Enter the new password.
  5. Confirm the connection.
  6. Wait for the printer to connect.
  7. Check the printer from your computer.

If the printer continues to fail, reset its network settings and configure WiFi again.

Canon Printer Won't Connect After Getting a New Router

A new router may have a different network name and password.

  1. Turn on the Canon printer.
  2. Open wireless settings.
  3. Select the new WiFi network.
  4. Enter the new password.
  5. Confirm the connection.
  6. Connect your computer to the same network.
  7. Add the Canon printer again if necessary.

Move the Printer

Place the printer closer to the router.

Check Router Security

Conclusion

When your Canon printer is not connecting to WiFi, the problem is often related to the wireless network, password, printer configuration, signal strength, or computer connection. Start by restarting the printer and router, checking the WiFi password, and making sure the printer and computer are connected to the same network.

To connect a Canon printer to WiFi, open the printer's wireless setup menu, select your network, enter the correct password, and wait for the connection to complete. If your printer and router support WPS, you can also use the WPS method.

If the printer connects to WiFi but your computer cannot find it, install the correct Canon driver, check firewall and VPN settings, and make sure both devices are on the same local network. For model-specific drivers, software, manuals, and setup information, use Canon Support .

Final Tip: With the correct wireless settings and compatible Canon software, most Canon WiFi connection problems can be resolved without needing a USB connection.
