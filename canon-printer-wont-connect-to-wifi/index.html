Canon Printer Won't Connect to WiFi? Try These Fixes

A Canon printer may fail to connect to WiFi because of an incorrect wireless password, weak signal, router problems, outdated network settings, or a communication issue between the printer and router. Fortunately, many wireless connection problems can be resolved with a few basic troubleshooting steps.

Why Won't My Canon Printer Connect to WiFi?

The most common reason is that the printer and wireless network are not communicating correctly. The printer may also be trying to use an old network configuration after you changed your router, WiFi name, or password.

Other possible causes include a weak wireless signal, incorrect network credentials, temporary router problems, or printer network settings that need to be configured again.

1. Check Your WiFi Connection

First, check whether other devices such as your phone or computer can connect to the same WiFi network. If other devices also cannot access the internet, the problem may be with your router or internet connection rather than the Canon printer.

2. Restart the Canon Printer

Turn off the Canon printer and wait for a short time. Turn it back on and allow it to finish starting up. A simple restart can clear temporary network communication problems.

3. Restart Your Wireless Router

If the printer still cannot connect, restart your wireless router. Turn the router off, wait briefly, and turn it back on. Wait until the WiFi network becomes available again before trying to connect the printer.

4. Check the WiFi Password

Verify that you are entering the correct WiFi password. Wireless passwords are case-sensitive, so an incorrect uppercase or lowercase character can prevent the printer from connecting.

5. Check the Distance Between Printer and Router

A weak wireless signal can cause connection problems. Move the Canon printer closer to the router during setup. Avoid placing the printer behind large objects or near devices that may interfere with the wireless signal.

6. Check the Printer's Wireless Settings

Open the network or wireless settings on the Canon printer. Check which WiFi network is selected and confirm that it matches the network currently used by your computer or mobile device.

7. Reconnect the Canon Printer to WiFi

If the printer was previously connected to another wireless network, configure the WiFi connection again. Select your current network, enter the correct password, and complete the wireless setup process.

8. Make Sure Your Computer Uses the Same Network

If you are trying to print from a Windows PC or Mac, make sure the computer and printer are connected to the same local network. Connecting the computer to a different network can prevent the printer from being detected.

9. Check for Network Changes

Think about whether your router, WiFi name, password, or network equipment was recently changed. A printer configured for an old network may need to be connected to the new network before it can communicate with your computer.

10. Try the WPS Connection Method

Some compatible Canon printers and wireless routers support WPS. If both devices support this method, you may be able to establish the wireless connection using the WPS option instead of manually entering the WiFi password.

Canon Printer Still Won't Connect to WiFi?

If your Canon printer continues to have connection problems after trying the steps above, review the printer's network settings and repeat the wireless setup process. You can also check the instructions specific to your printer model because available wireless features vary between models.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my Canon printer not connecting to WiFi?

An incorrect WiFi password, weak signal, router problem, or outdated wireless configuration can prevent a Canon printer from connecting.

Why did my Canon printer stop connecting after changing WiFi?

If your WiFi name or password changed, the printer may still have the previous network information saved. Reconfigure the printer using the new wireless network details.

Can restarting my router fix a Canon printer WiFi problem?

Restarting the router can resolve temporary network communication problems. After restarting it, wait until the WiFi network is available before reconnecting the printer.

Can I reconnect my Canon printer to WiFi?

Yes. Open the printer's wireless network settings, select your current WiFi network, enter the password, and complete the setup.
