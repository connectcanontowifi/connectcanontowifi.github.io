Canon Printer WiFi Setup: Step-by-Step Guide

Setting up a Canon printer over a wireless network allows you to print from computers, laptops, phones, and other compatible devices without using a USB cable. The exact wireless setup process can vary depending on your Canon printer model, but most models follow similar steps.

Before you begin: Make sure your wireless router is turned on and that you know the WiFi network name and password.

What You Need for Canon Printer WiFi Setup

Before starting the setup, place your Canon printer within a reasonable range of your wireless router. Check that the printer is powered on and that your computer or mobile device is connected to the WiFi network you want the printer to use.

  • A powered-on Canon printer
  • An active wireless router
  • Your WiFi network name
  • Your WiFi password
  • A computer, laptop, or compatible mobile device

How to Connect a Canon Printer to WiFi

Step 1: Turn On the Printer

Turn on your Canon printer and make sure it is ready for setup. If another device is already connected to the printer, you may need to cancel the current operation before beginning wireless configuration.

Step 2: Open the Wireless Setup Menu

Depending on your Canon printer model, open the printer's wireless or network settings from the control panel. Look for an option related to WiFi, wireless LAN, or network setup.

Step 3: Select Your WiFi Network

Allow the printer to search for available wireless networks. Select your WiFi network from the displayed list. Make sure you choose the correct network if multiple networks are available.

Step 4: Enter the WiFi Password

Enter your wireless network password carefully. WiFi passwords are usually case-sensitive, so check uppercase and lowercase letters before continuing.

Step 5: Complete the Connection

Confirm the wireless settings and allow the printer to connect to the network. When the connection is successful, the printer may display a WiFi icon, connected message, or similar indicator.

Connect Canon Printer to WiFi on Windows

After connecting the printer to your wireless network, add it to your Windows computer through the printer settings. Make sure the Windows computer and Canon printer are connected to the same network.

Open Windows Settings, go to Bluetooth & devices, select Printers & scanners, and choose the option to add a printer or scanner. Follow the instructions displayed by Windows.

Connect Canon Printer to WiFi on Mac

On a Mac, open System Settings and select Printers & Scanners. Choose the option to add a printer and select your Canon printer when it appears. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the setup.

Canon Printer WiFi Setup Using WPS

Some Canon printers and wireless routers support WPS. If your devices support this feature, you may be able to connect the printer without manually entering the WiFi password.

Open the wireless setup option on the printer, select the WPS method when available, and then press the WPS button on your compatible router. Follow the printer's instructions to complete the connection.

Canon Printer Won't Connect to WiFi

If your Canon printer cannot connect to WiFi, first verify that the router is working and that the WiFi password is correct. Restarting the printer and router can also resolve temporary network problems.

Check that the printer is within range of the wireless router. If the printer previously connected to another network, review its network settings and select your current WiFi network again.

How to Reconnect a Canon Printer to WiFi

If you recently changed your router, WiFi name, or wireless password, the printer may need to be configured again. Open the printer's wireless settings and select the new network. Enter the new password and complete the connection process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my Canon printer not finding my WiFi?

Check that the router is powered on and that the printer is within wireless range. Restarting the router and printer may also help.

Can I connect my Canon printer without a USB cable?

Yes. Compatible Canon printers can connect to a wireless network so that supported computers and devices can print without a USB connection.

Can I connect a Canon printer to a new WiFi network?

Yes. Open the printer's wireless network settings, select the new WiFi network, enter its password, and complete the setup.
