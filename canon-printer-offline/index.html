Canon Printer Offline? How to Fix It on Windows and Mac

Seeing your Canon printer listed as offline can be frustrating, especially when the printer is turned on and connected to your wireless network. A Canon printer can show an offline status because of a network communication problem, incorrect printer settings, a paused print queue, an outdated connection, or a temporary issue between the printer and computer.

Canon Printer Offline: Common Causes

A Canon printer may appear offline when the computer cannot properly communicate with it. The printer itself may still be connected to your wireless network, but the computer can temporarily lose contact with the printer.

Common causes include:

  • The printer is turned off or in an error state.
  • The printer and computer are using different networks.
  • The wireless connection is weak or unstable.
  • The printer was previously connected to another WiFi network.
  • The print queue is paused or stuck.
  • Windows or macOS is using an incorrect printer configuration.
  • A temporary network or communication problem has occurred.

Why Is My Canon Printer Offline?

If you are asking, "why is my Canon printer offline," first check whether the printer is powered on and whether it can communicate with the same network used by your computer. A printer can show offline even when its WiFi indicator appears active.

Restarting the printer and checking the wireless connection are good first steps. If the problem continues, review the printer settings on your computer and make sure the correct Canon printer is selected.

Canon Printer Offline but Connected to WiFi

A particularly common situation is a Canon printer offline but connected to WiFi. In this situation, the printer may successfully connect to the router while the computer still cannot communicate with it.

Why Is My Canon Printer Offline but Connected to WiFi?

The printer and computer may not be communicating correctly even though the printer has a wireless connection. The computer may also have an outdated printer entry or may be connected to a different network.

How to Fix Canon Printer Offline but Connected to WiFi

  1. Confirm that the computer and printer use the same WiFi network.
  2. Restart the Canon printer.
  3. Restart the wireless router if necessary.
  4. Check the printer status on your computer.
  5. Remove any paused or stuck print jobs.
  6. Try printing a test page.
  7. Reconnect the printer to WiFi if the network settings changed.

How to Fix Canon Printer Offline to Online

If you want to know how to fix Canon printer offline to online, start with the basic connection checks below. These steps can help restore communication between the printer and your computer.

Step 1: Check the Printer Power

Make sure the Canon printer is switched on. Check the printer display or indicator lights for any error message. Resolve any paper, cartridge, or hardware warning before continuing.

Step 2: Check the WiFi Connection

Confirm that the printer is connected to the intended wireless network. If you recently changed your router, WiFi name, or password, you may need to reconnect the printer to the new network.

Step 3: Restart the Printer

Turn the printer off, wait briefly, and turn it back on. Allow the printer to complete its startup process before checking its status on the computer again.

Step 4: Restart the Computer

Restarting the computer can refresh its network connection and printer services. After restarting, check whether the Canon printer is shown as online.

Step 5: Check the Print Queue

A stuck or paused print job can sometimes interfere with printing. Open the printer queue and check for documents that are waiting, paused, or showing an error.

Canon Printer Offline Windows 10

If you have a Canon printer offline Windows 10 problem, first verify that the printer and Windows 10 computer are connected to the same network.

Why Is My Canon Printer Offline Windows 10?

Windows 10 may display a printer as offline when it cannot communicate with the printer correctly. Network changes, printer configuration problems, or a stuck print queue can contribute to the issue.

How to Fix Canon Printer Offline Windows 10

  1. Open Windows Settings.
  2. Go to Devices and select Printers & scanners.
  3. Select your Canon printer.
  4. Open the printer queue and check for paused documents.
  5. Remove stuck print jobs if necessary.
  6. Confirm that the printer is connected to the correct WiFi network.
  7. Restart the printer and computer.
  8. Try printing a test document.

Canon Printer Offline Windows 11

If you have a Canon printer offline Windows 11 problem, Windows may be unable to communicate with the printer even though the printer is powered on.

Why Is My Canon Printer Offline Windows 11?

A Canon printer can appear offline in Windows 11 because of network communication problems, an incorrect printer configuration, a paused queue, or a temporary Windows printing issue.

How to Fix Canon Printer Offline Windows 11

  1. Open Windows Settings.
  2. Select Bluetooth & devices.
  3. Choose Printers & scanners.
  4. Select your Canon printer.
  5. Check its current printer status.
  6. Review and clear any stuck print jobs.
  7. Confirm that the printer and computer are on the same WiFi network.
  8. Restart both devices and try printing again.

Why Is My Canon Printer Offline on Mac?

If you are wondering why is my Canon printer offline Mac, check whether the printer is connected to the same wireless network as your Mac. A temporary network problem or an outdated printer connection can also cause the offline status.

If the printer was previously connected to another WiFi network, reconnect it to the current network before trying to print from your Mac.

How to Fix Canon Printer Offline on Mac

The following steps explain how to fix Canon printer offline on Mac and restore communication with the printer.

  1. Make sure the Canon printer is powered on.
  2. Confirm that your Mac and printer use the same WiFi network.
  3. Open System Settings on your Mac.
  4. Select Printers & Scanners.
  5. Check whether your Canon printer appears in the printer list.
  6. Review the printer status and any waiting print jobs.
  7. Restart the printer and Mac.
  8. Try printing a test document.

Canon Printer Offline Support and Help

If the basic troubleshooting steps do not resolve the problem, review the instructions for your specific Canon printer model. Different Canon models can have different wireless and network settings.

For additional Canon printer offline help, check whether your printer's wireless connection is stable and whether the computer recognizes the correct printer. If necessary, remove the printer from your computer and add it again using the appropriate wireless setup procedure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my Canon printer offline even though it is connected to WiFi?

The printer may be connected to the router but unable to communicate correctly with your computer. Check that both devices use the same network and restart the printer and computer.

How do I change my Canon printer from offline to online?

Check the printer power and WiFi connection, clear any stuck print jobs, restart the printer and computer, and confirm that the correct Canon printer is selected in your operating system.

Why is my Canon printer offline on Windows 10?

Windows 10 can display a Canon printer as offline when the computer cannot communicate with it. Check the network connection, printer settings, and print queue.

Why is my Canon printer offline on Windows 11?

A network problem, incorrect printer configuration, or stuck print queue can cause an offline status in Windows 11. Verify the WiFi connection and printer settings.

Why is my Canon printer offline on Mac?

Your Mac may not be communicating with the printer correctly. Confirm that both devices are on the same WiFi network and check the printer under System Settings and Printers & Scanners.

Can a Canon printer be offline while connected to WiFi?

Yes. A printer can have a WiFi connection to the router while the computer is unable to communicate with it. Checking the network and printer configuration can help resolve this situation.
